When budgets are tight and results need to be immediate, bottom-of-funnel (BOF) advertising consistently delivers the highest return on investment. Here's why BOF strategies should anchor your media plan.

## The BOF Advantage

Bottom-of-funnel tactics target consumers who are already in the market for your product or service. These are people actively searching, comparing, and preparing to purchase. The cost per conversion is typically lower because you're reaching people with existing intent.

## Key BOF Tactics

**Retargeting**: Re-engage users who've visited your site but didn't convert. These warm prospects convert at 2-3x the rate of cold audiences.

**Search Intent Targeting**: Reach consumers during their research phase with programmatic ads triggered by search behavior signals.

**In-Market Audiences**: Leverage data signals that indicate active purchase consideration in your category.

**Dynamic Creative**: Serve personalized ads featuring the specific products or services each prospect has shown interest in.

## Balancing the Funnel

While BOF deserves its "top dog" status for immediate ROI, a complete strategy includes top and mid-funnel investment to continuously fill the pipeline. The most efficient campaigns maintain a healthy balance — typically 60% awareness, 20% consideration, and 20% conversion-focused.
